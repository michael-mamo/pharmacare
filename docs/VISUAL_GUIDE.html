1. Getting to know your screen

Every page has the same three parts. Once you know these, you can find anything.

The three parts of every screen
  1. The menu (left, black)Your list of pages. You only see pages your job needs. The yellow bar shows where you are now. On a phone, tap the ☰ button to open it.
  2. The top bar🌐 changes the language (English / አማርኛ). 🔔 shows your alerts — a red number means unread. Your name on the right is where you sign out.
  3. The main areaWhatever you are working on right now.

2. Signing in

First time signing in? You will be asked to create your own password straight away. This is normal — it means nobody else knows it, not even your manager. Use at least 10 characters with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a symbol.
Wrong password 5 times? The system locks for 30 minutes to keep your account safe. Wait, or ask your Administrator.
After signing in you land on the page you use most: Cashiers and Pharmacists → the till. Store Managers → Inventory. Administrators → Dashboard.

3. Selling something (the till)

This is the Point of Sale page. Left side: find the item. Right side: the basket and payment.

The till, with the four things you touch
  1. Scan or typePoint the scanner at the barcode — the item drops into the basket by itself. Or type part of the name and pick from the list.
  2. Check the itemUnder each name you see what it is (Medicine, Medical Device, Cosmetic…) and how many are left. A red Rx tag means a prescription is needed.
  3. Adjust the basketChange qty for more than one. Add a disc % for a discount. The ✕ removes a line. Totals update by themselves.
  4. Take paymentPick the customer (or leave Walk-in), choose Cash / Card / Mobile Money, type what they handed you — the change appears. Then press Complete Sale.
You cannot oversell. If you try to sell more than you have, the system stops you and tells you exactly how many are left. Stock drops the moment the sale finishes.
Need the receipt again? Menu → Sales → find the invoice → press the receipt icon → Print.

4. What you can sell — not just medicines

The counter sells more than tablets, so everything lives in one catalogue. Each item has a type.

Things that never expire — a knee brace or a BP monitor — simply have no expiry date, and they are never included in expiry warnings. Anything you swallow or put on skin must have an expiry date; the system will insist on one.

5. Receiving a delivery Store Manager

Menu → Purchases → Record Purchase.

One row to start — press + Add Item for each extra product
  1. Pick the supplier and dateFrom the delivery note.
  2. Fill the first rowProduct, how many arrived, and cost each for this delivery.
  3. Only if the price to customers is changingType the new figure in New selling price (the yellow box). Leave it empty to keep the current price.
  4. Press + Add Item for each extra productAdd as many rows as the delivery note has. The invoice total adds up as you type.
  5. Save Purchase & Update StockStock goes up straight away.
Check before you save. A purchase cannot be edited afterwards, because it is a money record. If you get it wrong, an Administrator must delete it (which also puts the stock back) and you record it again.

6. How profit is worked out

This is the part people ask about most, so here it is in pictures.

Older stock is counted at the older cost — exactly as it should be
The three things worth knowing
  • Oldest-expiry stock sells first. The system always takes from whatever expires soonest, so nothing sits and goes out of date.
  • Old stock keeps its old cost. Buying more at a higher price never changes the profit on sales you already made.
  • VAT is not profit. VAT is collected for the tax office, so it is left out of the profit figure.
Want to see the batches? Menu → Inventory → Batches. You will see each delivery, its cost, and how many are left. (Cost figures are visible to Administrators and Store Managers only.)

7. Checking stock and expiry

Menu → Inventory. The four coloured boxes at the top are buttons — press one to see that list.

The device has a dash for expiry — it does not expire, and that is fine

If something has expired

  1. Take it off the shelfRight away. Do not dispense it.
  2. Tell the Store ManagerThey record a Stock Out with the reason "expired write-off", so the count matches the shelf.
  3. Check what else is closeInventory → Expired → the Within 30 days tab.
